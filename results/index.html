Results, certificates and reports

Results are declared on the dates announced for each programme. Certificates and reports are available to download once results are out.

Download your certificate or report

Enter your roll number on the results portal to download your certificate and, where applicable, your performance-analysis report.

Open the results portal

Result dates for 2026-27

AMO Level 1May 2027 (Level 2 in June; final results July 2027)
REAL OlympiadMay 2027
RMQJune 2027, with reports
UNITEWithin four weeks of each school's exam

Physical certificates and awards

Students who register through their school receive a physical certificate at the school. Medals, trophies and rank awards are dispatched to the school or the registered address as per programme rules.

Have a question about a result?

Write to support@unlockiqinstitute.com within 15 days of the result declaration with the roll number and details. Our grievance officer reviews every result-related concern.
