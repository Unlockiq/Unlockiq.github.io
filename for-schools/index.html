Academic enrichment programmes for schools

Unlock IQ partners with Indian and international schools to conduct academically structured olympiads, quizzes and assessments for Classes 1–11 — online, flexibly scheduled, light on your staff, and followed by reports your teachers can use.

UNITE Olympiad

Maths and reasoning, scheduled with your school in November, December or January. Trophies for class toppers, medals for achievers, a certificate for every participant.

How coordination works

  • One coordinator tracks registrations and attempts on a dashboard.
  • Your school gets its own exam window.
  • Parent consent comes first.
  • Students attempt online on any device.

What your school receives

  • Student results and certificates.
  • A class-wise performance summary.
  • Trophies and medals, delivered to the school.
  • A dedicated support contact.
