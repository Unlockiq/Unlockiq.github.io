About Unlock IQ Institute

We are a Pune-based education company that believes every child can think mathematically — given the right questions, honest feedback and a little encouragement.

What we do

Since 2022, Unlock IQ Institute Pvt. Ltd. has conducted online olympiads, quizzes and assessments for students of Classes 1–11: the Aryabhatta Maths Olympiad, the REAL Olympiad, the Ramanujan Maths Quiz and the UNITE Olympiad — along with digital practice papers released class by class for Classes 1–11 and a growing range of printed study material. Our papers are mapped to the NCERT classroom syllabus and written to reward application over recall.

How we work

  • Academic first. Every paper is reviewed for accuracy, age-appropriateness and fairness before it reaches a student.
  • Reports, not just ranks. Results come with feedback a parent or teacher can act on.
  • Consent-first. Children's data is processed only with parental consent, kept to the minimum, and never used for advertising.
