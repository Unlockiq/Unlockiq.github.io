How our papers are different

Many olympiads test how fast a child can recall. Ours test how well a child can think. Three principles shape every Unlock IQ paper:

  • Same-class topics, greater depth. Questions stay within the child's own classroom syllabus but ask for application, connection and multi-step reasoning rather than one-step recall.
  • Every question teaches something. Papers are designed so that reviewing them afterwards is itself a learning exercise, with stepwise solutions in our practice material.
  • Success is structured, not scarce. From performance certificates to distinction medals to trophies, recognition is layered so effort at every level is acknowledged — while top honours remain genuinely earned.
