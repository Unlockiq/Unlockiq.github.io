Inter-school maths & reasoning olympiad

UNITE Olympiad 2026-27

To bring the olympiad experience into the school itself: an inter-school and inter-group maths and reasoning event, scheduled around each school's calendar, that celebrates achievers at every level.

Bring UNITE to your schoolSee how UNITE works
EligibilityClasses 1–11
Exam dateNov / Dec / Jan
FormatScheduled with each school

Who it is for

Schools and groups of schools (or coaching groups) for students of Classes 1–11. Individual students participate through their school.

How the exam works

  • Organised in November, December or January on a date chosen with each participating school or group.
  • One online exam featuring questions from Mathematics and the Intelligence Test (reasoning).
  • A flexible 12-hour window on the scheduled day; attempt on any common device.
  • Proctored to retain the highest standards of fairness. A school-level performance summary accompanies individual results.

Awards and recognition

Trophies for toppersFor the topper in each class.
Gold, silver and bronze medalsGold for 95% and above, silver for 85% and above, bronze for 75% and above.
CertificateEvery participant receives a certificate according to their performance in the exam.

All awards are given as per programme rules.
