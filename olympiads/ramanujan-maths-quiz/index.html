Ramanujan Maths Quiz 2026-27

To build mathematical mastery through a series of tests, and to give every child — and their teachers — a detailed diagnostic picture of strengths and areas to improve.

Register for RMQView RMQ syllabus
EligibilityClasses 1–11
Exam date1–7 May 2027
Format3–5 tests per level

Who it is for

Students of Classes 1–11 who want steady improvement with clear feedback — a strong preparation for AMO.

How the exam works

  • Level 1 (Class 1): three tests. Levels 2–11: five tests each, spread across 1–7 May 2027.
  • Each test is online and multiple-choice; students attempt all tests of their registered level.
  • A comprehensive performance-analysis report is issued after the series is complete.
  • The report helps teachers and parents identify and address the student's areas for improvement in mathematics.

Awards and recognition

Medal of ExcellenceFor students scoring at least 80% in every test of their registered level.
Performance-analysis reportEvery participant receives a detailed, topic-wise analysis report.
CertificateA participation certificate for every student.

All awards are given as per programme rules.

Frequently asked questions

What does 'per level' mean?

A level corresponds to a class. Registration is ₹400 per student for all tests of that level.

How many tests are there?

Three for Level 1; five each for Levels 2 to 11.

What is in the report?

A topic-wise analysis of performance across all tests, showing strengths and specific areas to work on.

When are results and reports shared?

After the full series is complete, as per the programme schedule.
