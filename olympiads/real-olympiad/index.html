REAL Olympiad 2026-27

To develop and measure the reasoning, logic and aptitude skills that no single school subject teaches — but every competitive path demands.

Register for REALPractice papers
EligibilityClasses 1–11
Exam date18 Apr 2027
FormatSingle stage

Who it is for

Students of Classes 1–11, whatever curriculum they follow. REAL builds reasoning skills, so it complements any board.

How the exam works

  • Single stage — online, on Sunday 18 April 2027, with a flexible 24-hour window (IST).
  • Multiple-choice questions in verbal and non-verbal reasoning, graded from familiar to challenging.
  • One complimentary mock exam for practice.
  • Proctored to ensure a fair and secure testing environment. Attempt on any common device.

Awards and recognition

Medal of DistinctionFor every student scoring 85% and above.
CertificateEvery participant receives a certificate according to their performance in the exam; school-route students receive a physical copy.
Performance feedbackScore and percentage shared with every participant after results.

All awards are given as per programme rules.

Frequently asked questions

Is REAL a maths exam?

No — it is a reasoning and logic exam. It complements maths olympiads by testing thinking skills rather than curriculum knowledge.

When is it held?

Sunday, 18 April 2027, with a 24-hour window so students can attempt at a convenient time.

What is the fee?

₹250 per student, including one mock exam.

Is there a study material?

Yes — REAL Olympiad practice papers and class-wise books are available in our shop.
