How our papers are different

Many olympiads test how fast a child can recall. Ours test how well a child can think.

  • Same-class topics, greater depth. Questions stay within the child's own classroom syllabus but ask for application and multi-step reasoning rather than one-step recall.
  • Every question teaches something. Reviewing a paper afterwards is itself a learning exercise, with stepwise solutions in our practice material.
  • Success is structured, not scarce. Certificates, medals and trophies are layered so effort at every level is acknowledged, while top honours remain genuinely earned.
