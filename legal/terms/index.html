Olympiad and quiz exams are conducted online and may be proctored, as stated on the relevant programme page; other assessments may use different supervision arrangements. Our decision on integrity matters is final. Technical issues on our side that prevent an attempt are handled by offering a re-attempt. Fees are non-refundable; see the no-refund policy.
