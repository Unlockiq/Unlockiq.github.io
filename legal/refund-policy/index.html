Refund, Cancellation & Shipping Policy

Clear rules for every kind of purchase on this site.

Exam registrations

  • You may cancel and receive a full refund up to 7 days before the Level-1 / exam start date of the programme.
  • Between 7 days and the exam start, a 50% refund applies.
  • No refund once the exam window has opened or the student has attempted a mock test on the platform.
  • If we cancel or are unable to conduct an exam, you receive a full refund.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7–10 working days of approval.

Digital products (practice papers, activity books, worksheets)

  • Personalised PDF practice papers are generated and delivered instantly and are non-refundable once downloaded, because each copy is personalised to the buyer.
  • If a download link fails or the file is corrupt, write to us within 48 hours with your payment ID and we will re-issue the link at no charge.
  • Each child's file can be downloaded up to 3 times. The download link works for 7 days and can be reopened any time via "Find my order" with the order ID and mobile number.

Printed products (books, games, bookmarks)

  • Dispatched within 3 working days of payment (pre-orders: as per the date shown on the product page).
  • Delivery across India by courier, typically 4–8 working days. Tracking details are sent on dispatch.
  • Damaged or wrong items: tell us within 48 hours of delivery with photos; we replace at no charge.
  • Change of mind: return unused, unopened items within 7 days of delivery; refund minus the shipping cost.

Schools

School-level programmes (UNITE, diagnostic assessments) are invoiced and refunded as per the school agreement.

How to request a refund
