Children's Privacy Notice & Parental Consent

A plain-language notice for parents and guardians, and the consent we ask for before a child takes part in any Unlock IQ programme.

Why we ask for your consent

Many participants in AMO, REAL Olympiad, Ramanujan Maths Quiz and UNITE are under 18. Indian law (the DPDP Act, 2023) requires consent from a parent or lawful guardian before a child's personal data is processed. Where a participant is under 18, registration and the processing of their data are therefore handled through the parent or lawful guardian (or through the school with the parent's consent) — never by the child alone.

What we collect about your child

  • Name, class, school and city (to run the exam and issue the certificate)
  • Exam responses and scores (to compute results and ranks)
  • For proctored exams only: limited exam-integrity data — camera snapshots taken by the exam platform during the exam and a record of tab-switches (see "Proctoring during exams" below)

What we do not collect

  • No location tracking, no biometric data, no social-media profiles.
  • No photographs or video outside a proctored exam, and none used for any purpose other than reviewing exam integrity.

What we do not do

  • We do not track your child across the internet or serve them advertising.
  • We do not sell or share your child's data with advertisers.
  • We do not contact your child directly — all communication goes to the parent's registered mobile and email.
  • We do not publish results with names, except a top-ranks list. If you do not want your child's name published, tell us and we will leave it out.

Proctoring during exams

Olympiad and quiz exams that are proctored (as stated on each programme page) use the exam platform to take periodic snapshots via the device camera during the exam only, and to record tab-switches. This data is used only to review exam-integrity concerns. These are stored for 90 days for the purpose of reviewing any integrity concern, then deleted. Registration includes consent to this proctoring; if you do not agree, please do not register.

Your rights as a parent

You may, at any time, see the data we hold about your child, correct it, withdraw consent (which ends the child's participation), or ask us to delete it once the programme is complete. Contact our grievance officer: Mr. Swami S., privacy@unlockiqinstitute.com.

The consent we record

At registration, the parent or guardian ticks a box confirming that they have read this notice and consent to the processing described. We record the time, the account and the version of this notice. For school-based registrations, the school collects the same consent on our printable form and keeps it on file.
